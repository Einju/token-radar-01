Research & Methodology Notes

1. Fitted Valuation Curve

The token radar model establishes a baseline valuation framework for pre-token protocols by regressing historical market capitalization against Total Value Locked (TVL) across 129 traded comparables. The logarithmic regression yields the published formula: log10(mcap) = 3.025 + 0.522 × log10(TVL). This sub-linear exponent (0.522) reflects diminishing marginal market cap returns as protocol TVL scales to extreme magnitudes.

2. Held-Out Tests & Error Metrics

Model robustness was rigorously validated using Leave-One-Out (LOO) cross-validation and split-sample testing. The LOO median absolute log error is 0.5468 (translating to a multiplicative factor error of approximately 3.5×), with 32.8% of predictions falling within a 2× error band. Split-sample validation confirms consistent out-of-sample error characteristics (0.5425 median absolute log error).

3. Token Odds & Growth Multipliers

Raw baseline valuations are adjusted using protocol-specific factors including longevity (age in days), chain footprint breadth, category risk weights, and recent 7-day TVL momentum. Token odds represent the estimated empirical probability of successful token launch and airdrop distribution based on historic category behavior and funding milestones.

4. Verdict Classification Formula

Protocols are classified into actionable verdicts based on expected value (EV) per $1,000 farmed: EV = implied_value_usd × 0.05 (assumed airdrop share) × (1000 / 10,000,000) (farmer share of $10M total farmed capital) = implied × 5e-6. Assumptions: 5% airdrop allocation, $10M total farmed capital pool, $1,000 farmer allocation. FARM when EV > $50 (implied > $10M; expected ~22 protocols); THIN when $10 ≤ EV ≤ $50 (implied $2M–$10M; expected ~131 protocols); and SKIP when EV < $10 (implied < $2M; expected ~47 protocols).

5. Key Model Limitations

An R² of 0.194 demonstrates that TVL alone explains less than 20% of valuation variance in crypto assets. Governance structure, tokenomics utility design, team reputation, and macro liquidity conditions exert massive influence not captured purely on-chain. Token odds are rough logistic approximations, and estimated farm step costs are point-in-time reflections.

6. Snapshot & Market Volatility Notice

All data within this radar is snapshotted as of 2026-11-14 via DefiLlama and associated indexers. Crypto markets exhibit extreme volatility; TVL figures, ranking positions, and implied valuations shift rapidly in live trading environments. Use this dashboard as an analytical decision-support tool rather than financial advice.
